## About this game

You are one cuttlefish, hatched alone on a coral reef, roughly the size of a
thumbnail. Nothing is guarding you. You have a handful of months to live, which
is the truth about cuttlefish, and in that time you have to eat enough to grow,
stay unseen by everything that hunts, dig a home out of the sand, and if you
manage all of that, leave a clutch of eggs fastened to your own ceiling.

Then you play your own heir on the same reef, inheriting the water your parent
left behind — including the algae they never cleared and the strength they built
or spent.

> **Hatch. Hunt. Hide. Hand it down.**

Most lives end without a clutch. That is the honest version, and the game does
not hide it.

It is a quiet, watchful game rather than a fast one. Camouflage matters more than
speed. Nothing here can be fought head-on and won.

## Features

**A reef that isn't the same twice**
A 144-megapixel world generated fresh each life, built from 40 biome patches —
seagrass meadow, sand flats, rocky reef, kelp forest, coral garden — each with
its own density. Some meadows are thin and sandy; others are a thicket that hides
you completely and rewards searching. The card in the corner names which kind
you're standing in.

**Sixty-odd species, each with its own behaviour**
Sharks that hunt by electric field. Groupers that hang still and inhale prey
whole. Damselfish that farm an algae patch and drive off anything that nears.
Cleaner wrasses whose stations hold a truce even predators respect. Conches that
pole-vault across night sand and sometimes turn up a buried clam.

**Cuttlefish things, done properly**
Drink the reef's colour and vanish in plain sight. Blow water through the sand
and sink until only your eyes show, then ambush what wanders past. Loose a cloud
of ink shaped like yourself and bolt in the same breath.

**A home you dig yourself**
Take a cave from its tenant, or hollow out your own scrape on open sand. Line it
with weed to hide it, stone to guard the eggs, shell to brood them healthy,
driftwood to hold the roof against silt. Mount your rarest finds on the wall.

**A line that remembers**
Court a mate in flashes of skin-light, lead them home, and lay. Generations
later your heir can find the ruin of its own birth-den at the reef's edge and dig
your keepsakes out of the silt — with a record of who carried them and for how
long.

**A reef that responds**
Water temperature, pH and algal cover all move, and all have consequences. Let
algae climb and the coral pales toward bleaching. Clear the litter, weed the
bloom, pry the crown-of-thorns off the coral, and the water is better than you
found it.

**Things to find**
39 achievements across five categories, plus 9 hidden ones. 14 unlockable
guises, each a different real cuttlefish or squid species with its own body and
colouring. 7 inherited instincts that awaken only when you've gathered the right
field notes. A field guide of 55 observations that fills in as you live them.

## Controls

Laptop keys by default. Touch controls are a toggle in Settings.

| Key | |
|---|---|
| **WASD** | swim |
| **Space** *or* left-click | strike |
| **Shift** | jet (hold) |
| **E** *or* right-click | hide |
| **C** | grab / interact |
| **X** | bury in sand |
| **B** | dig a den (hold) |
| **V** | ink |
| **R** | skin-signal |
| **Q** · **M** · **I** · **H** | tasks · map · carrying · field guide |
| **hold Tab** | full readout — vitals, water, tasks, every binding |

The always-on display is two cards. Bottom-left is your body: hearts, food, jet,
camouflage, ink, age. Top-right is the water: where you are, the day track,
temperature, pH, algae. Everything else waits behind **Tab**.

**On a phone:** turn on touch controls in Settings and hold the phone sideways.
Touch anywhere on the left half to swim — the stick appears under your thumb.

## Requirements

Any current browser — Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge. Nothing to install.

- **Desktop:** works on anything from the last decade. Frame times measured at
  2.5 ms median, so there is a great deal of headroom.
- **Phone / tablet:** works in landscape. Turn on touch controls.
- **Sound:** browsers block audio until your first click. That is normal.
- **Saves:** stored in your browser, tied to the address. Four save slots.
- **Offline:** works once loaded, apart from the web fonts.

## Accessibility & options

Four difficulty levels from *sheltered* (hunters almost never notice you) to
*harsh*. Adjustable life length from about 15 minutes to a whole evening. Three
text sizes. Reduced visual effects. Separate music and effects volumes. Weather
and disasters can each be switched off. Heredity can be set from realistic to
forgiving to off. Every key rebindable. The tutorial can be skipped.

## On the biology

Field notes are checked against current science, and where the game takes a
liberty **the note says so plainly**:

- Cuttlefish do not dig their own dens. Octopuses are the diggers. Digging is
  something we added so a life has somewhere of its own to end up.
- Cuttlefish have no light organs. The glow you cast at night is ours, not theirs.
- Cuttlefish have no planktonic larval stage. A hatchling is a miniature adult
  and belongs to the seafloor from its first breath.

Everything else — the three hearts, the blue-green blood, the colour-blind eye
that makes the sea's finest colours, the mucus cocoons parrotfish sleep in — is
real, and cited as such. Corrections are welcome; open an issue.
